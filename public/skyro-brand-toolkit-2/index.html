Internal 4.2 Banned Growth Mechanics

Artificial urgency

No countdown timers on financial decisions.

Social proof pressure

No "4,000 people took this loan today."

Guilt-driven re-engagement

No "you haven't saved this week."

Friction to leave

Closing must be as easy as opening.

Hidden upsells

No pre-checked boxes or buried terms.

Gamification of debt

No streaks for borrowing, no badges for spending.
