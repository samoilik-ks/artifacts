The Philippine Communication Calendar

The financial rhythms of Filipino life are predictable — use that predictability to be present at the right time, with the right message.

PeriodPriorityTone and Angle
January — New Year, enrollment prepHighNew start energy, practical. "The year starts now. What are you working toward?"
February — Valentine'sMediumLight, warm, Filipino-style romantic humor. "The best gift is the one you can actually give."
April — Holy Week (Semana Santa)Low / CarefulRespect the period. Minimal product push. If communicating, tone should be reflective and quiet.
June–August — Enrollment seasonHighEducation as family investment. OFW angle. Concrete, practical support.
1st–7th & 16th–22nd every month — Pre-payday windowConstantThe week before payday when money runs thin. Short-term loan offers framed around the gap: "Payday is a week away. We can help you bridge it." Calm, practical, zero pressure.
15th and 30th every month — PaydayConstantEnergetic, celebratory, specific offers. The brand that's always there on payday.

Channel Guidance

One voice, different registers — depending on the platform and the moment.

Facebook 95% penetration — the primary platform ▼

The non-negotiable channel. Taglish mandatory for all emotionally significant content. Long-form family and merchant stories outperform short product posts. Engagement rates of 4–7% are achievable — use interactive content, questions, and genuine storytelling. Post daily; quality is more important than frequency, but consistency builds brand recognition.

TikTok 80%+ and growing — the Gen Z channel ▼

Maximum energy, minimum script. Slice-of-life format. Real product demos through real-scenario storytelling. The rule: if it sounds like it was written in a brief, it doesn't belong on TikTok. Trending sounds are fine; the Filipino context has to be real. Finance content creators are growing fast — find them, understand them, work with them authentically.

YouTube Top 5 globally per capita — the storytelling channel ▼

Long-form is not a liability here — it is an opportunity. Documentary-style merchant stories, OFW family reunion content, financial literacy series with genuine utility. Invest in production quality for this channel; it is where considered, durable brand content lives.

Television 67% daily viewers — the mass trust channel ▼

Still the most powerful trust-transfer medium in the Philippines, particularly outside Metro Manila. Prime time 7–9 PM. Celebrity ambassador format performs best. Emotional, family-centered narratives. This is where Skyro builds mass-market credibility — the kind that earns sustained customer relationships.

In-store / POS ▼

Minimum text, maximum clarity. Show what the product does in the moment of decision. QR codes that lead to a fast, seamless experience. The physical presence of Skyro branding in a trusted merchant location is itself a trust signal — treat it as one. Branding should feel like it belongs in the store, not like it was placed there.

Push notifications and SMS ▼

Ultra-short, concrete, and genuinely useful — or they will be disabled. Payday reminders work. Payment due notices with clear options work. "Exciting offer inside!" does not. The rule: if it contains nothing the user could not have learned more quickly themselves, do not send it.

Customer support ▼

The most human channel in the Skyro ecosystem. A support interaction gone wrong can undo months of brand-building. A support interaction done right — warm, specific, respectful, with a real resolution — creates advocacy that no campaign can buy. Rejections and difficult messages require the most care: specific reason, specific path forward, no judgment.
