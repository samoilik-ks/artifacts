Communication Examples

All examples below are written in English to communicate intended meaning, tone, and register. In actual execution, Taglish is the recommended default for mass-market and emotionally resonant communications. Local copywriters should adapt the spirit, not just translate the words.

The Tagline in Market-Specific Communications — "Naturally, It's Yours"

The most effective approach: lead with the single most relevant product truth or cultural insight for the Filipino market, then close with Naturally, it's yours.

Approach 1 — Lead with a cultural or emotional insight

ExampleYou send love home every month. Naturally, it's yours.
ExamplePayday on the 15th. Payday on the 30th. Finance that keeps up. Naturally, it's yours.
ExampleFrom your sari-sari store to something bigger. Naturally, it's yours.
ExampleFor the family at home and the one working far from it. Naturally, it's yours.

Approach 2 — Lead with a specific product offer

ExampleApproved in minutes. No paperwork, no branch. Naturally, it's yours.
ExampleBuy it today. Pay on your next payday. Naturally, it's yours.
Example0% interest for the first 3 months. Naturally, it's yours.
ExampleTake it home today. Sort the rest over 24 months. Naturally, it's yours.
