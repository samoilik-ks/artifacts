⚠️ Before you use this document — two important notes:

1. The world is not static. This guide reflects market intelligence, cultural dynamics, and competitive context as of Q1 2026. The Philippines' digital finance landscape is evolving rapidly — new players, new regulations, new fraud patterns, new cultural moments. No guide can substitute for critical thinking. Before publishing any communication that feels complex, sensitive, or on the edge of what's described here — double-check it. Run it past the local team. Apply judgment. The rules in this document are a foundation, not a ceiling.

2. Language of this document. All copy examples and guidelines are written in English so the entire global and local team can read, validate, and give feedback on meaning and tone. Actual execution should be adapted into Taglish for mass-market and emotionally resonant communications — the closer a message is to the emotional core of the brand, the more Filipino the language should be. Regional languages — Cebuano, Ilonggo, Bisaya — for provincial market targeting. Translate the spirit, not just the words.

How to Use This Document

This guide is built for the Skyro Philippines team. Its purpose is to show how the global brand platform translates, sounds, and behaves specifically on this market — not to replace the Global TOV Guide or the master brand, but to sit on top of them as a layer of cultural interpretation.

The core principle: we don't change the brand to fit the market. We find where the brand and the market already meet — and amplify that intersection.
