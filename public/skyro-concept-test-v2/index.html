Delta vs Iteration 1 — What Changed

✓ Fixed

  • «Shop like a boss» removed — the slogan most criticised in v1 is gone from every new asset
  • Onboarding now in Philippines format — ₱ 3,000 instead of RM 10,000, Filipino faces in frame
  • «For loved ones» elder is now SEA — Filipino / Malay archetype instead of Western
  • «Buy now, pay later» woman is Asian — reads as local for PH / MY
  • «Buy goods with no fees» — two Asian friends on a tropical backdrop instead of LA-skyline
  • «Naturally, it's yours» — comma added, reads more breathable

✗ Still Broken

  • App icon — same diagonal stripes, still mistaken for sport / logistics
  • No halal / Shariah signal anywhere — KSA and MY Muslim audience still locked out
  • No hijab on any woman in frame — Fatima, Siti still can't see themselves
  • No M-Pesa integration visible anywhere — Kenya mass market still outside
  • No Kenyan or Saudi faces — only SEA representation
  • No B2B / SME track — Wei Chen, Khalid still asking for it

+ Newly Introduced

  • Skyro Visa Card — virtual card with S-curve design. First time the product is shown as a physical object
  • Banner 17 hero shot — young Asian man in a creative studio (freelancer / creator archetype)
  • Magenta / pink accent — appears in Banner 17 alongside electric blue. New colour signal
  • Standalone OOH «Buy now, pay later» — pure brand poster, no people. Reads as confident
  • Malaysia onboarding lost in switch to PH — new risk for MY market positioning

Saudi Arabia
Avg score: 6.0 / 10+0.1
Marginal improvement, driven by two things: «Shop like a boss» is gone (Ahmed and Siti's most-cited rejection trigger), and the Skyro Visa card finally gives Mohammed a concrete product object to evaluate. But the structural gaps remain unchanged: no halal badge, no hijab, no Saudi faces, no SAR onboarding. Khalid moves up to 7.5 thanks to the card. Fatima holds at 5.5 — she noticed the slogan was removed but the women still don't wear hijab. KSA needs its own localization pass, not just slogan hygiene.
🇲🇾
Malaysia
Avg score: 5.9 / 10−0.3
The only market that dropped. Reason: the Malaysia-specific onboarding (RM 10,000, TouchnGo-relevant QR pay) was removed in favour of Philippines localization. Ahmad's 7.5 in v1 was almost entirely driven by that flow — he drops to 6.5 («where did my RM screens go?»). Siti gains slightly from «Shop like a boss» removal but loses ground without MY localization. Wei Chen unchanged — B2B is still invisible. Net: the concept's MY win in v1 was a localization win, not a brand win. Lose the localization, lose the score.
🇵🇭
Philippines
Avg score: 7.2 / 10+1.2
The biggest jump in the study. Three changes lifted the entire PH segment: (1) ₱ 3,000 onboarding amount finally relevant to DC3 (Driver, Sari-Sari, Call Center Agent move from rejection to engagement); (2) Filipino faces on the onboarding screens — Teacher, Nurse, Housewife say «this is us»; (3) the elder for «For loved ones» now reads as a lolo / lola figure. OFW holds at 9.0, Content Creator at 8.8, but the real story is the lift across the DC3 mass: average DC3 score went from 4.5 to 5.8. Government Employee still skeptical — regulatory credentials remain the unsolved problem for cautious borrowers.

🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia — v2 Findings

6 personas · Avg: 6.0 / 10 +0.1 vs v1

DELTA
Two upgrades, three structural gaps unchanged. «Shop like a boss» removed (Ahmed +0.4, Siti-type effect). Skyro Visa card now visible (Mohammed +0.4, Khalid +0.6). But halal, hijab, Saudi faces, SAR onboarding — all unchanged.
VISA CARD WIN
Mohammed (banker): «Finally a tangible product». The Visa card on the OOH gave him the first concrete artifact to evaluate. He still wants product differentiation, but the brand crossed from «aesthetic» into «product» in his eyes.
FATIMA HOLDS
Fatima 5.5 → 5.5. The «Shop like a boss» relief is real, but it's exactly offset by the fact that the same women in frame still wear no hijab. She named both changes unprompted in the same minute.
RAJESH STILL OUT
Rajesh 4.4 → 4.5. The PH onboarding is irrelevant to him — he\'s an Indian expat in KSA. The concept still has no remittance-to-India scenario and no micro-loan flow. v2 didn't address his use case.
RECOMMENDATION
(1) Ship KSA-specific onboarding set: SAR amounts, Sharia-compliant savings, Salam financing. (2) Halal badge on Visa card and on all KSA creatives. (3) Saudi-cast photo: family at iftar, abaya, father-daughter. (4) Add a remittance-to-India scenario for the expat segment.

🇲🇾 Malaysia — v2 Findings

3 personas · Avg: 5.9 / 10 −0.3 vs v1

DELTA
The only market that lost ground. Reason is mechanical: the RM 10,000 + QR pay onboarding flow was replaced by the PH version. Ahmad's v1 score (7.1) was almost entirely a localization-driven win. Without it, he drops to 6.5.
LOCALIZATION LESSON
This is the cleanest evidence in the study that localized money screens drive scores. Show MY their RM, MY moves up. Take it away, MY moves down. Same logic must be applied to KE (KES) and KSA (SAR) — and parallel onboarding kits must be maintained, not swapped.
SHARIAH STILL ABSENT
Three respondents, three unsolved Shariah questions. Ahmad: «Last mile — Shariah-compliance». Siti: «Where\'s the halal badge?». Even Wei Chen, who isn\'t Muslim, notes: «If you launch without it, you lose 60% of the customer base».
SLOGAN RELIEF
«Shop like a boss» removal helped Siti +0.5 on emotion. She was the most vocal v1 critic of that line. v2 feels less hostile to her values even without other changes.
RECOMMENDATION
(1) Restore MY onboarding (RM, QR pay) in parallel with PH version. (2) Shariah-compliance badge — mandatory before launch. (3) Photoshoot with Malay, Chinese, Indian faces. (4) SME track for Wei Chen segment — separate onboarding flow.

🇵🇭 Philippines — v2 Findings

17 personas · Avg: 7.2 / 10 +1.2 vs v1

DELTA
+1.2 — the largest single-market lift in the study. Three precise interventions: ₱ 3,000 onboarding, Filipino faces on onboarding screens, SEA elder for «For loved ones». DC3 average went from 4.5 to 5.8.
DC3 BREAKTHROUGH
Driver 5.5 (+0.9), Sari-Sari 6.0 (+1.2), Call Center 7.5 (+1.2). ₱ 3,000 is a credible nano-loan amount for daily-income earners. The previous RM 10,000 read as «not for me» — now «that\'s exactly my Friday emergency».
FILIPINO FACES LANDED
Teacher PH +1.0, Housewife +0.6, Nurse +0.9. «I see myself» moved these personas from neutral to engaged. The lola figure under the sunset sky is consistently named as the emotional peak of v2.
OFW UNCHANGED
OFW holds at 9.0. Already the top scorer in v1. «For loved ones · Send money to kinfolks with no fees» is still the most powerful line in the entire study. Should remain the central PH campaign.
REGULATORY GAP
Government Employee 4.5 → 4.5. Faces and currency don\'t close his gap. He needs BSP / SEC credentials in visible place — a credentials footer on creatives and onboarding.
RECOMMENDATION
(1) Replicate the PH playbook for KE (KES, M-Pesa, Kenyan faces) and KSA (SAR, hijab, halal). (2) Hero the OFW «For loved ones» line as the central PH campaign. (3) Add BSP / SEC credentials footer. (4) Add nano-loan flow (₱ 500 - ₱ 3,000) prominently — it\'s the DC3 acquisition wedge.

🇰🇪 Kenya — v2 Findings

10 personas · Avg: 5.7 / 10 +0.3 vs v1

DELTA
Modest lift, accidental driver. The warmer SEA cast is more emotionally relatable than v1's global stock — even though no Kenyan face appears. Brian Otieno and Faith Wambui benefited directly from Banner 17 and the Visa card. But the mass market gap is unchanged.
PH ONBOARDING = NEGATIVE FOR KE
Kimani, Akinyi, Kamau, Odhiambo all flagged: «If the onboarding is for Philippines, it\'s not for us yet». The PH-only localization in v2 is a regression for KE mass market — it makes their absence more pronounced.
VISA CARD = FAITH\'S MOMENT
Faith Wambui 7.4 → 8.0. «Now I can put this next to an iPhone». The Skyro Visa card is exactly the asset she\'d display in her electronics shop. She is the natural KE Gen Z ambassador if M-Pesa lands.
BRIAN HEROES THE BANNER
Brian 8.5 → 9.0+. Banner 17 (young Asian man in creative studio) is read by Brian as «that\'s my life, that\'s my generation». Even with no Kenyan face, the energy and posture match Nairobi Gen Z hustler-creator identity.
M-PESA STILL ENTRY TICKET
9 of 10 respondents asked about M-Pesa in the first minute. Unchanged from v1. No M-Pesa = no trust = no scale. The Visa card without M-Pesa pairing is a partial story for Kenya.
RECOMMENDATION
(1) Ship KE onboarding kit (KES + M-Pesa green tick) in parallel — same priority as PH version. (2) Local photoshoot: boda boda, mama mboga, duka, Nairobi skyline. (3) Pair Skyro Visa card with M-Pesa integration message on KE creatives. (4) Brian / Faith as first-wave KE ambassadors.
