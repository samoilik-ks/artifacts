Positioning principle
Skyro helps people move forward on their terms, not under pressure.
A product earns its own name only if it carries its own ideology. If it describes what the product does — descriptor. If it carries a belief — manifesto. The five variables (icon · name · typeface · color · weight) measure distance from the masterbrand. More variables changed = more autonomy = more ideology. Changing variables without a substantive reason is not permitted — this is not a design system, it is a system of arguments.
Below is the brand architecture grouped by channel type: B2C, B2B2C, B2B, and B2E. Each product card includes markets of presence and five brand variables: icon, naming, typeface, colour, and weight.
